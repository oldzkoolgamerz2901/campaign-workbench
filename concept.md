# campaign-workbench 2.0

The campaign workbench is a way to help DM's create their campaigns. World-building, session preparation, session playing, and player handouts


## domain
- Scene
- Location
- Arfifact
- Obstacle
- NPC
- Enemy (same as NPC?)
- item
- Weapon
- Spell
- Vehicle
- ...

## Fundament: 4 functionalities

### campaign writer
- Assists a DM with world building and scene writing
- Create vast worlds while keeping oversight and consistency
- the campaign workbench provides templates for all domain types to guide with writing
- Build in AI will work as a co-author. From brainstorming ideas to naming suggestions or planning out a battle
- The AI will be guided by a custom made harness

### session planner
- Document are tagged as scene texts, items, enemies, npcs
- the DM can select and sort a collection of documents and add notes. TBD relations, for example a gang of enemies, or an item carried by an NPC. alternative is written notes
- TBD: maps

### Session playing
- All domain entities will be represented as cards
- The app provides a display where the cards can sorted and layed out, stacked how the DM wants to
- TBD: a map where cards can be linked on?

### player handouts
- cards can be shared, possibly with differen or partial information. So an NPC can be shown to the players, a map, items can be handed out

## Possible technique and methods
- MD's as basics with tags
- Images as assets
- A session can be started. MD's with matching images will be turned into HTML cards
- These cards can be picked up, dragged and stacked to designated places on the screen.
